About this map

Markers show current PM2.5 (µg/m³) from every monitor in Bali we can reach. Colors follow US EPA breakpoints. Wind layer (when enabled) shows 10 m surface wind from Open-Meteo — useful for understanding which direction smoke from a fire would drift. Map is locked to Bali; scroll and pan freely, click a pin to open full detail.

Sources: PurpleAir · AQICN / KLHK · IQAir contributors · Airly / Nafas · OpenAQ / AirGradient (historical). Wind data © Open-Meteo. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors.
