§ VI — The Data Review

Ninety days of data — on the island's garbage fires.

Two thousand sensor-days across seven networks, read end to end. The record keeps house hours — a spike at nine in the morning, a second climb after dark, everywhere on the island at once. That is the schedule of household waste fires, not of any harvest. And this dry season is running dirtier than last year's.
